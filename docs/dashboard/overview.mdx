The Overview answers "is everything fine?" over a window of time: the last hour, 24 hours, 7 or 30 days. Every figure,
bar and row opens the runs behind it in [Runs](/dashboard/runs).

| Figure | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Runs started** | Runs started in the window, against the previous window |
| **Completed** | The share of the runs that ended which completed |
| **Failed** | Failed runs, plus the aborted and cancelled ones |
| **Waiting now** | Runs waiting on the outside world, and how many wait on a signal |
| **Duration p95** | The 95th and 50th percentiles of the duration of ended runs |
| **Quarantined** | Runs held until an operator releases them or gives them up |

Below them:

- **Runs started, by status**: completed, failed, cancelled and aborted, and those still queued, running or waiting,
  over the window.
- **What fails most**: failed runs by fingerprint, the error with its numbers, ids and strings replaced, so the same
  failure across many different workflows is one row.
- **Failing actions**: the actions whose calls failed in the window.

The figures come from hourly counters, kept per end user: the query bar at the top narrows them to one end user,
`namespace:user_ada`, and the organization's are shown otherwise. Every other term of a
[query](/dashboard/query-language), such as `@agent:triage`, applies to the runs a figure opens, and the query and the
window follow you into Runs. Counters carry no labels: the figures are not available to a
member limited to labels.
