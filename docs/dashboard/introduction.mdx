The dashboard shows what every script did: each run with its source, its calls and its history, the failures grouped
across the many scripts models write, the tools' latency and errors, and the use of each end user. It reads the same API
as the SDK, so what the dashboard shows is what your harness and your model see.

| Where | |
| --- | --- |
| The hosted service | [app.rewind.codes](https://app.rewind.codes) |
| A server of your own | The server's own URL: `rewind server` serves the dashboard at the root (see [Self-hosting](/self-hosting)) |

## Sign in

On [app.rewind.codes](https://app.rewind.codes), sign in with **Continue with GitHub**, or with your email and the
6-digit code sent to it. Then pick your organization, or create one; an invitation link takes you straight to the
organization that invited you. Sign-ups go through a waitlist: someone new chooses **Join the waitlist** on the sign-in
page and, once approved, gets an email and signs in with that address.

## Find your way

The dashboard has one sidebar:

- **At its top**: the organization picker, and **Find** (press <kbd>F</kbd>): a run by its handle or its id, a view, the runs a query matches.
- **The views**: [Overview](/dashboard/overview), [Observability](/dashboard/observability), [Scripts](/dashboard/scripts),
  [Testing](/dashboard/testing), [Tools](/dashboard/tools) and [Namespaces](/dashboard/namespaces); then
  [Connect Claude](/claude), to use Rewind from Claude.
- **At its bottom**, the account menu: your [organization](/dashboard/organization) (members, roles, API keys,
  connections), your [connections](/claude#change-or-revoke-a-connection), the theme, the time zone (UTC or local), the
  server, and sign out.

Observability, a run and the organization turn the sidebar into their own, under a **back** arrow: from a run, back
returns to the list it was opened from.

## What you see depends on your role

The dashboard shows what your role allows. The run views need `runs:read`, Testing `toolsets:read`, the namespace
views `namespaces:read`, and each part of the organization its own permission. A member limited to labels
(`agent:triage`) sees only the runs carrying them. See [Roles](/dashboard/organization#roles).

The dashboard never picks an end user: it reads every namespace of your organization. A run's namespace is a column, a
filter and the term `namespace:` of the [query language](/dashboard/query-language).

## Live

The dashboard follows your organization's runs as they change: a run that completes, fails or starts waiting shows it
in every table and panel at once, the lists and counts follow within seconds, and a run that is still going follows its
event stream as each step is committed. While the dashboard reconnects, the views read again every 10 to 30 seconds.

Nothing on the screen moves on its own. A row changes its values in place; when new runs arrive or the order changes
while you are scrolled down a table, they wait behind **N new · Show** at the top of the table, and enter at once when
you are at its top. Changing a search keeps the previous rows, dimmed, until the new ones arrive.

Links carry the view, the query, the window and the step, so a URL you share opens the same thing.
