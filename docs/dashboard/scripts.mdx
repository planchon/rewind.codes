title: "Scripts"
description: "Every distinct script your models wrote, with its runs, its outcomes, its tools and its labels."
---

Scripts are content-addressed: two runs of the same source are runs of one script. The Scripts view lists every distinct
script executed, the most recently run first.

Each row shows the script's first lines and digest, its runs and their outcomes, the tools it called and the labels of its
runs. Search them as on the [Overview](/dashboard/overview), in one line of text: `@agent:triage` keeps the scripts that ran
with a label (repeat the term to require several), `tool:github.close_issue` those that called a tool. A click on a row's
tool or label adds it to the search.

Open a script to read its full source, its tests and its runs. Its tests are the suites of the workflow versions it is
the script of, as [Testing](/dashboard/testing#a-workflow) shows them. From a run, the **Script** detail opens the same
page.

Use it to see what your models actually write: which scripts run most, which fail, and which tools they reach for.
