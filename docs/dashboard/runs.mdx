Runs holds the runs of your organization in four views of the same query: **All**, **Running**, **Waiting** and
**Failures**. Its sidebar holds the views and the filters.

## The query bar

Type a [query](/dashboard/query-language): terms separated by spaces, all of which must hold. The bar suggests fields
and the values that exist as you type (after `namespace:`, the end users whose name starts with what you typed), and
underlines a term the server cannot read.

## Filters

The sidebar lists, for each field, the values found with their counts: the timeline, the status, the workflows, the
namespaces, each label, the actions, the errors. A filter is a term of the query:

- one click asks for the value (`status:failed`);
- a second click excludes it (`-status:failed`);
- a third removes it.

## The four views

### All

The runs started over time, then the list: each run with its handle, its status, its namespace, its workflow, when it
started, its duration, its calls, when it wakes and its error. Click a header to order the runs by its column (newest
or oldest first, latest activity, longest, most calls, soonest to wake) or to filter what it shows: a status, a
namespace, a workflow, a window of start, a duration, a number of calls, the actions called, an error, a label. A
header's filter is a term of the query, so it shows in the query bar and is saved with a search.

A run's status is `queued`, `running` or `waiting` until it ends `completed`, `failed`, `cancelled` or `aborted`. A
waiting run shows what it waits for on hover.

### Running

The runs with a step in progress, or a call Rewind is making itself (to an MCP server, an API or `http`) in flight or
backing off.

### Waiting

The runs waiting on the outside world, grouped by what they wait for, by tab: **signals**, **timers**, **harness
actions**, **approvals**, **deferred** and **parked** calls, and **quarantines**. Each run shows the line it is paused
on and when it wakes, the soonest first. Use it to find what a person, your worker or your harness has to answer.

### Failures

The failed, cancelled and aborted runs of a window, by **fingerprint**: the error with its numbers, ids, times and
quoted strings replaced, so the same failure across the many workflows models write is one row. Each fingerprint shows
its runs over time, the workflows that hit it, when it was first and last seen, the line most of its runs ended at,
and its latest run by its handle. Its scope is one end user (`namespace:`) or the organization, over whole hours, as
the Overview's.
