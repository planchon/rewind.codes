Open a run from anywhere: a row of Observability, a figure of the Overview, Find with its id. Its sidebar holds its
status and id, its sections, and its details: namespace, workflow and version, the run that started it, script, toolset,
calls, trace id and labels. A run that is still going updates live, step by step.

## Debugger

The debugger is the source of the script, with what each line caused beside it: tool calls with their duration, log
lines, timers, signal waits. Above it, the **timeline** shows each call, timer and signal wait as a span, with idle time
compressed. When the run waits, a banner says for what, at which line, and how to answer.

### The time cursor

A run's history is a sequence of **steps**: something from outside (the start, a tool's result, a timer, a signal, a
cancellation) and what the script did in response. The cursor steps through them like a debugger:

- **Step back**, **Step**, first and last, or the <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> keys. **every step** also stops on the
  steps that change nothing in the source.
- At each step, the source marks the awaits that just resumed, the calls issued, those still open and those done. The
  panel on the right says why the run woke up, what it did, and where it is paused.
- **Hover** an underlined call to see its arguments and result as known at that step, or a variable bound to an awaited
  call (`const issues = await github.list_issues(…)`), or `input`, to see its value. **Click** to pin it as a watch.
- The step is in the URL (`?step=`): a link opens the run at that moment. On a live run, the cursor follows the head.

  Only values that crossed the journal are known: inputs, results, signals, and the variables bound to an awaited call.

Payloads show their size, digest and preview; their full value loads on demand.

## Actions

At the top right:

- **Forks**: the runs forked from this one, such as the `retry_run` of a failed run.
- **Journal**: downloads the run's journal document, which replays on its own (`rewind replay journal.json`).
- **Cassette**: downloads its calls and signals, for [tests](/sdk/testing#test-against-a-real-run).

**Back** returns to the list of Observability the run was opened from, with its query.
